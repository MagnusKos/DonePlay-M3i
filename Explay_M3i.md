## Just a bit of research about the Explay M3i that I own.

It works, information screen says:
- **Firmware version**: 30.30.0048
- **Firmware date**: 2007.09.30

On the back lid it has a sticker with serial number:
- **S/N**: 2G43070804507

And now internals.
